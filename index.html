SpotMe Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

SpotMe ("SpotMe", "we", "us", or "our") is a workout accountability app that lets you and your friends keep a shared workout streak. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and an email address (which may be Apple's private relay address) along with a unique account identifier.
  • Workout and fitness data. The workouts you log (exercises, muscle groups, cardio, and dates), your streaks, workout plans, saved presets, and any protein entries you choose to record.
  • Team data. The team you create or join, your display name, and the workout activity you share with your teammates.
  • Subscription information. When you subscribe, we receive your subscription status and purchase history from Apple and our subscription provider. We never receive your full payment card details. Apple processes all payments.
  • Assistant messages. If you use the in-app assistant, the messages you send may be processed to generate a response (see "Service providers" below).
  • Notifications. If you enable reminders, we store a device push token so we can deliver them.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app: track your streak, show your team's progress, generate your workout plan, and send the reminders you enable.
  • To manage your subscription and access.
  • To answer your questions through the in-app assistant.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools or track you across other apps or websites.

Service providers

We share data with the following providers only so they can perform services for us. They may use your information solely to provide those services to us and are required to protect it in a manner consistent with this policy:

  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, payment processing, and push notification delivery.
  • Supabase for database and authentication hosting (stores your account, team, and workout data).
  • RevenueCat for subscription management.
  • Groq for processing in-app assistant messages when the cloud assistant is used. When the on-device assistant is used, your messages are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers.

These providers may store and process your information in the United States, which may have data protection laws different from those in your country. By using SpotMe, you consent to this transfer.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Settings, then Delete account), which removes your account and the data associated with it. After deletion, some information may remain in backups for a limited period, or be retained by our providers or as required by law (for example, Apple's record of your purchases).

Your choices

  • Turn reminders on or off in the app, or disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Delete your account in the app at any time to remove your data and withdraw consent.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

SpotMe is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and the right not to be treated differently for exercising those rights. You can exercise most of these directly in the app, or contact us using the email below and we will respond as required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of SpotMe after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at mylesdrewbiz@gmail.com.
